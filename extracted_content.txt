CLEARING & FORWARDING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (SRD)
Kiswahili – English Working Version / Technical Brief
Document ya kumpa ICT Specialist kwa ajili ya kutengeneza mfumo wa Clearing & Forwarding
1. PURPOSE YA MFUMO
Nataka mfumo ambao utasimamia business yote ya Clearing & Forwarding kuanzia mteja anapowasilisha documents, kufunguliwa file, assessment & declaration, duty payment, physical verification, Delivery Order, Release Order, port charges, loading, transport mpaka cargo/container kufikishwa kwa customer na Job kufungwa.
Mfumo usiwe database ya kuhifadhi taarifa tu. Uwe ni Management & Control System ambao CEO/Management anaweza kuingia na kuona kwa wakati huo shipment iko wapi, nani anahusika, imekaa siku ngapi, nini kimekwama, gharama zilizotumika, mteja amelipa kiasi gani na Job imeleta profit kiasi gani.
2. MAIN OBJECTIVES
Kila shipment/container iwe na unique Job Number.
Kufuatilia shipment step-by-step mpaka delivery.
Kila stage iwe na Responsible Person.
Kurecord Start Date/Time, Completion Date/Time, Status, Remarks na Supporting Documents.
Kusimamia revenue, expenses, approvals na client payments.
CEO kupata live dashboard bila kutegemea taarifa za mtu kwa simu/WhatsApp.
Field/Port staff waweze ku-update system kwa kutumia mobile phone.
Kuwa na complete audit trail ya kila action na change.
Kutoa reports za Operations, Finance, Clients na Profitability.

5. DETAILS ZA KILA STAGE
5.1 RECEIVED DOCUMENTS
Register Client na shipment.
System itengeneze Job Number automatically.
Capture BL, Vessel, Container, Cargo na Origin information.
Upload documents.
Record date/time received na staff aliyepokea.
System ionyeshe missing documents kama zipo.
5.2 OPENING FILE
Digital file ifunguliwe chini ya Job Number.
Documents zote zihifadhiwe kwenye file moja.
Record file opening date na responsible officer.
Ordinary user asiweze kufuta file bila permission.
5.3 ASSESSMENT & DECLARATION
Record declaration/assessment number.
Record assessed duty na relevant charges.
Track submitted/completed status.
Upload supporting documents.
System ionyeshe siku zilizotumika against target ya 1–3 days.
5.4 DUTY PAYMENT
Record amount payable, amount paid, payment date na payment reference.
Upload receipt/evidence ya payment.
Show outstanding balance.
Next controlled stage isiruke kama payment confirmation bado haijapatikana.
5.5 PHYSICAL VERIFICATION
Record vessel arrival date.
Record date file forwarded.
Assign responsible officer.
Record verification date, status na remarks.
Field officer aweze kupakia photo/document kwa mobile.
5.6 DELIVERY ORDER
Record application date, DO number, charges na date received.
Track PENDING / APPLIED / RECEIVED.
Record responsible officer na remarks.
5.7 RELEASE ORDER
Record request date na receipt date.
Record Release Order number.
Record responsible officer.
System ifungue next stage baada ya completion.
5.8 PORT INVOICE
Record invoice number, date, amount na due date.
Link invoice na Job Number.
Record payment status.
5.9 PORT CHARGES PAYMENT
Record amount payable na amount paid.
Record payment date/reference.
Upload receipt.
Expense i-linkwe na Job Number.
5.10 GATE PASS, LOADING & TRANSPORT
Record Gate Pass number.
Assign truck na driver.
Record container number, seal number, pickup date/time.
Record departure na destination.
Port/field officer aweze ku-update kwa mobile.
5.11 DELIVERY & JOB CLOSURE
Record expected na actual delivery date.
Upload Proof of Delivery (POD).
Record customer acknowledgement/signature/photo where applicable.
Job iwe CLOSED/COMPLETED baada ya required conditions kukamilika.
Historia yote ibaki kwenye system kwa audit na future reference.

8. FINANCE, APPROVALS & INTERNAL CONTROL
Nataka system iwe na control ya matumizi. Expense isiwe tu ina-recordiwa; pale inapohitajika ipitie approval kulingana na authority level.
Kila expense inayohusiana na shipment lazima iunganishwe na Job Number.
System iwe na configurable approval limits.
Expense request ionyeshe Requester, Job Number, Purpose, Amount, Supporting Document, Approver, Approval Date na Payment Status.
Status ziwe REQUESTED, APPROVED, REJECTED, PAID au CANCELLED.
Approval limits ziweze kubadilishwa na management bila developer kubadilisha code.
Mfano wa approval matrix — amounts hizi ni mfano na zitathibitishwa na management:

20. VERY IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE
Sitaki mfumo ambao una-record data tu. Nataka mfumo wa kusimamia business nzima ya Clearing & Forwarding. CEO aweze kufungua dashboard na kujua: shipment iko wapi, nani anahusika, imechukua muda gani, nini kimekwama, imetumia kiasi gani, client amelipa kiasi gani na Job ina profit kiasi gani.
Kwa kila stage mfumo capture: Responsible Person + Start Date/Time + Completion Date/Time + Status + Remarks + Supporting Document + Approval (where required).
